The Finer

Details

We can’t wait to celebrate our special day with you! Here’s everything you need to know to make the most of the weekend

Ceremony of Love

Our ceremony will begin at 3:00pm in the glasshouse of Tantalus Estate. Please arrive at 2:30pm to settle in

After we say “I do,” we’ll celebrate with drinks & canapés in the gardens at cocktail hour, before moving inside for dinner, speeches, and dancing the night away!

Our Day at a Glance

Transport

On the day, return transport will be arranged from central Oneroa and the ferry wharf to Tantalus Estate and back after the reception

Travel + Stay

Waiheke Island is just a short 35-minute ferry ride from Auckland City with Fullers360 ferries running regularly to Matiatia Wharf. From there, it’s a quick 10-minute drive to Oneroa or 15 minutes to Tantalus Estate

We recommend booking accommodation in Oneroa for convenience — there are plenty of boutique stays, hotels, and holiday rentals to choose from

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I let you know I have dietary requirements?

Don’t worry, on the day you’ll be able to choose from Mudbrick’s fresh seasonal menu. Just let us know your dietary needs when you RSVP

May I bring a date or plus-one?

Our wedding is by invitation only — and due to capacity limits, only the guests listed on your invitation envelope are invited to attend

Are little ones invited?

We kindly ask that our special day be reserved for adults only. We hope you enjoy the night off & have fun with us!

Is the venue outdoors?

The ceremony and cocktail hour take place outdoors with the reception indoors. As evenings can get chilly, we recommend bringing something warm

If I arrive early, what can I do?

Mudbrick has a beautiful outdoor area amongst their vines that’s open to the public — the perfect spot before the ceremony

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, there are plenty of spaces to park your car overnight
